It routes on capability

Thirteen agents score every facility on what it can treat tonight — cath lab, stroke unit, trauma grade, ICU — not on how far away it is.

02

It explains what it rejected

The runner-up is named, the factor it won on is named, and the cost in road minutes is stated. The dispatcher overrides with full sight of the trade.

It measures outcome, not minutes

Time to definitive treatment against the guideline target — door-to-balloon 90 min, door-to-needle 60 — including the secondary transfer a closer ER would force.
